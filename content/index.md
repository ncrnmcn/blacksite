## 🧟‍♂️ **THE TURN STRUCTURE**

### 1. **Wake Up**

John regains consciousness somewhere in the mall (or a new scene).  
The GM describes his surroundings — flickering lights, panicked survivors, etc.

### 2. **Bidding for Control**

Each player secretly chooses how much **Willpower** they spend to seize control.

- Everyone reveals simultaneously.
    
- Highest bidder wins.
    
- All others lose the Willpower they bid.
    
- Winner loses only **1** Willpower (regardless of bid).
    

If a tie occurs, roll a die.

---

### 3. **In Control**

The winning voice narrates John’s actions until:

- He fails a roll,
    
- He takes damage,
    
- Or something traumatic happens (e.g., limb loss, flashbacks, uninvited karaoke).
    

At that point, new bidding begins.

---

## 🎲 **ROLLS**

When John tries something risky, roll **1d6**:

- **1–2** = Fail horribly (brains everywhere).
    
- **3–4** = Partial success (messy).
    
- **5–6** = Success! (questionably heroic).
    

Modifiers:

- +1 if it fits your voice’s personality or obsession.
    
- -1 if it’s absurdly outside zombie capabilities (e.g., playing violin with missing fingers).
    

---

## 💀 **WILLPOWER**

- Spend **1** to take control during bidding.
    
- Earn **1** whenever your current obsession is _progressed_.
    
- Earn **3** when your obsession is _completed_.
    
- When out of Willpower, your voice goes silent (or gets eaten by the others).
    

---

## 🩸 **DEATH & MAYHEM**

If John’s body dies (again), the GM may:

- Respawn him somewhere else in the mall.
    
- Start a new day with new obsessions unlocked.
    
- Or end the session in a glorious symphony of moans and escalator jingles.
    

---

## 🧃 **ZOMBIE MALL FLAVOR (Optional Rules)**

|Variant|Description|
|---|---|
|🧠 **Brain Buffet**|Eating a brain restores 1 Willpower to _all_ voices (shared high).|
|💬 **Echoes of the Lab**|Once per session, a voice can remember a flash of John’s human past for narrative chaos.|
|🦴 **Loose Limbs**|If John loses a body part, that limb can act independently for 1 round — possessed by the last losing bidder.|
|📣 **Mall Announcements**|GM can interrupt play at any time with absurd mall PA messages to trigger new bidding rounds.|
